Horizon Player is a lightweight, high-performance video and music player for Android powered by mpv and FFmpeg with a modern Material 3 / Liquid Glass interface.

Features:
- mpv native core with hardware decoding (mediacodec) and wide codec support
- Full ASS, SSA, SRT, and PGS subtitle styling
- Gesture controls for brightness, volume, and seeking
- Picture-in-Picture & background audio playback
- Dedicated Movies, Series, and Music library with MediaInfo format badges
- Synchronized lyrics support and multi-pane tablet layout
- 100% private with zero telemetry
