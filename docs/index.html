Cinema & Hi-Res Music,

Horizon Player is a private, ad-free Android media player powered by mpv and ffmpeg. Enjoy 10-bit HDR video, lossless FLAC audio, synced lyrics, and a fluid Material 3 Expressive interface.

Download Latest APK View Source on GitHub

Built for Media Enthusiasts

Everything you need for local video, movies, and lossless music.

🎬 Pro Video Engine

Powered by mpv + libplacebo with HW+ decoding, 10-bit HDR support, portrait & landscape PiP, and SVG format badges.

🎵 Hi-Res Audio & Lyrics

Dedicated music player with FLAC/ALAC/Dolby badges, Audio Pulsing spotlight, A-B loop, equalizer, and synced lyrics.

🔮 Liquid Glass & Privacy

100% offline-first and ad-free. Includes a biometric Secure Folder vault, OLED dark theme, and customizable layouts.
