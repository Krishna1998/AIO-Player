Cinema & Hi-Res Music,
Reimagined in Liquid Glass.

Horizon Player is a private, 100% offline-first Android media player built on mpv, FFmpeg, and libplacebo. Experience 10-bit HDR video, lossless FLAC audio, synced lyrics, and a fluid Material 3 Expressive interface with zero ads or tracking.

Designed for Power & Elegance

Every detail—from hardware-accelerated decoding to frosted Liquid Glass controls—is crafted for speed and clarity.

🎬

Pro Video Engine & Format Badges

Powered by mpv + libplacebo with HW+ Direct decoding, 10-bit HDR, portrait & landscape PiP, and crisp SVG format badges (MP4, H.265/AV1, 10-bit, AAC/5.1, SRT) across Continue Watching, Movie Details, and Player Controls.

🎵

Hi-Res Music Player & Synced Lyrics

A dedicated music experience featuring Audio Pulsing spotlight cards, SVG quality badges (HI-RES, FLAC, DOLBY), A-B loop, 10-band equalizer, sleep timer, and real-time synced embedded or online lyrics.

🍿

Smart Movies & Series Hub

Automatically organizes movies and multi-episode series with poster artwork, watch progress bars, episode lists, and direct resume—while filtering out non-movie clips.

🛡️

Biometric Vault & Custom Optics

Lock private folders behind a biometric Secure Folder vault. Customize Liquid Glass optics, OLED dark themes, wallpapers, gestures, and player control layouts.
